Common Questions

Is my data really private?

Yes. All calculations run in your browser using JavaScript. We don't have a database. We don't use cookies for tracking. We don't even have a server that processes your inputs. If you clear your browser data, your history disappears. That's the point.

Are these tools accurate?

They're as accurate as the formulas they use — which are the same formulas doctors and researchers use. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Body fat estimators (like the Navy method) have a margin of error of ±3%. We show you the uncertainty, not just the number.

Why is this free?

I built these tools for myself first. Then I figured other people might find them useful. The site is supported by advertising (clearly labeled) and affiliate links to products I actually use. No paywalls, no "premium features," no data selling.
