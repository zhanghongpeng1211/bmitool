Protein Quality Note

Target 1.6-2.2 g/kg body weight for muscle building, 1.2-1.6 g/kg for maintenance, 1.0-1.2 g/kg for general health. Animal proteins (meat, eggs, dairy) have higher bioavailability than most plant proteins. If vegetarian, aim for the higher end of the range.

How It Works

This planner converts your daily calorie target into specific gram targets for protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Here is how the math works:

The Calorie-to-Gram Conversion

Each macronutrient has a fixed calorie density:

Protein = 4 calories per gram
Carbohydrates = 4 calories per gram
Fat = 9 calories per gram

For example, if your target is 2,200 calories with a 30/40/30 split:

  • Protein: 2,200 × 30% = 660 cal ÷ 4 = 165g
  • Carbs: 2,200 × 40% = 880 cal ÷ 4 = 220g
  • Fat: 2,200 × 30% = 660 cal ÷ 9 = 73g

Preset Templates Explained

Each preset is optimized for a different goal:

  • Balanced (30/40/30): Good for general health and maintenance. Moderate protein supports muscle preservation without over-restriction.
  • Low Carb (35/15/50): Higher fat helps satiety. Useful for insulin sensitivity or appetite control.
  • Keto (20/5/75): Very low carb induces ketosis. Requires strict adherence and is not suitable for high-intensity athletes.
  • High Protein (40/30/30): Maximizes muscle protein synthesis. Best for strength training and body recomposition.
  • Athlete (25/55/20): Carb-heavy to fuel glycogen stores. Ideal for endurance runners, cyclists, and team sports.

Protein Per Kilogram

The tool calculates your protein intake relative to body weight. Research-backed targets:

  • 1.0–1.2 g/kg: General health, sedentary adults
  • 1.2–1.6 g/kg: Maintenance with light activity
  • 1.6–2.2 g/kg: Muscle building, strength training
  • 2.0–2.4 g/kg: Competitive bodybuilders during cuts

Practical Tips

  • Start with the preset closest to your goal, then adjust sliders to match your preferences.
  • Ensure percentages add to exactly 100% — the tool will warn you if they do not.
  • Meal breakdown assumes 4 meals/day. Adjust portions if you eat 2–3 larger meals or 5–6 smaller ones.
  • Save multiple plans to compare. All data stays in your browser.
