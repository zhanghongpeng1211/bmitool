What I Can Help With

  • Questions about how the tools work or how to interpret your results
  • Bug reports (please include your browser and device — "it broke" is not helpful)
  • Suggestions for new tools or features
  • Corrections to articles (I make mistakes — tell me and I'll fix them)
  • Sharing your own health data story (I love featuring great stories from the community)

What I Cannot Help With

  • Medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations
  • Personalized diet or exercise plans
  • One-on-one coaching or consultation
  • Guest posts, sponsored content, or SEO partnerships
  • Link exchanges or "digital PR" requests

We do not offer professional services or consultation. If you would like to share your story, feel free to email us — we love featuring great stories from our community.
